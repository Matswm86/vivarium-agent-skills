## Plant freely, drop the Salvinia

Multiple independent sources agree, and the exception is specific:

- "Geosesarma vampire crabs **do not gnaw on plants**" — suitable for planted setups; they eat dead and decaying plant matter only.
- *G. dennerle* is called "one of the best crabs for a planted tank because they won't bother live plants."
- "Won't damage plants; will consume dead matter."
- **Documented exception: crabs have been observed catching and eating *Salvinia*** (a floating plant) in captivity.

**Keeper-safe plants named in sources:** bromeliads, creeping fig (*Ficus pumila*), and emersed-grown anubias, cryptocoryne, swords, and common stem plants in the water section.

**Verdict: plant freely. Omit floating Salvinia from the crab zone.**

Note this sits alongside the separate warning that *Ficus pumila* overruns enclosures — the crabs will not eat it, but it still needs pruning (see `flora-fast-growers-bury-slow-ones.md`).

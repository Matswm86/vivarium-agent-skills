## You get 10-20 minutes, so solve everything first

Silicone skins in roughly 10-20 minutes. Every pane must be pre-cut, pre-cleaned, pre-taped, and dry-fitted before a cartridge is opened.

**People and space:**
- A 1500 x 1600 x 12 mm low-iron pane weighs **~72 kg** (2.4 m2 x 0.012 m x 2500 kg/m3, my calc) and stands 1.6 m tall. **Three people minimum** — two to carry and place, one to gun silicone and hold squares. Do not attempt alone.
- A 1700 x 600 x 8 mm pane is ~20 kg (my calc) but long and whippy. Two people.
- Assemble **on the final stand, or on a dead-flat table you can walk all the way around**. Never on carpet or any flexing surface.

**Phase 0 — dry fit, the day before:**
1. Unpack and inspect every pane for edge chips. **A chipped edge is a crack initiator — reject the pane.**
2. Dry-assemble the whole box with tape and clamps, no silicone. Check diagonals equal within 2 mm, check the bottom pane drops in, check every gap.
3. Mark the bond footprint on each pane with pencil or fine tape. Photograph the dry fit.

**Phase 1 — surface prep:**
4. Degrease every bonding surface with **isopropanol or acetone** on lint-free cloth, wiping in one direction, changing cloth faces. Two passes.
5. Let the solvent flash off completely, 5-10 minutes. Any residue kills adhesion.
6. Do not touch bonded areas with bare fingers afterwards.

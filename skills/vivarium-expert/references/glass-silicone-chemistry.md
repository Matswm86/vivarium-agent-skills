## Acetoxy is the aquarium standard, not neutral cure

A widespread belief holds that "neutral cure is the safe one for animals." For structural glass-to-glass seams that is backwards.

| Cure type | By-product | Glass adhesion | Aquarium use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Acetoxy (acetic)** | acetic acid (vinegar smell) | best unprimed grab on glass | **industry standard for aquarium seams** |
| Neutral / oxime | oxime | better on porous substrates; may need primer on bare glass | not the aquarium norm |
| Alkoxy | methanol | — | no aquarium-branded product found; UNVERIFIED |

Every explicitly aquarium-branded European product found is acetoxy: Soudal Silirub AQ, Ottoseal S 28, Ramsauer 410 Aquarium, DOWSIL Glass Silicone Aquarium Sealant. The acetic acid is a **cure by-product that is gone once cured** — Soudal state directly that although Silirub AQ is acetic, "the product is not poisonous to animals after curing."

**What actually makes silicone unsafe:** fungicide and mould-inhibitor additives in "sanitary", "bathroom", "kitchen", and "anti-mould" grades. That is the hazard flag, not the cure chemistry.

**"100% silicone" on the tube is NOT a safety guarantee.** A product can be 100% silicone polymer and still carry a biocide package. The safety signals are an explicit aquarium/terrarium rating and, ideally, a cited fish-toxicity test.

**Products carrying real certification:**

| Product | Certification |
|---|---|
| **Ramsauer 410 Aquarium** | DIN 32622, ÖNORM B 5014-1, **OECD 236** fish test, NSF S1, ISEGA food-contact declaration, EMICODE EC1PLUS |
| **Ottoseal S 28** | DIN 32622 pt 4.4.2.2, **non-toxic to fish per OECD 203** (Institut Fresenius), EN 13501 class E |
| Soudal Silirub AQ | DIN 32622, EC1+; rated for all-glass aquaria up to 200 x 60 x 60 cm |

**Verify:** Look for a DIN 32622 reference or a named OECD fish-toxicity test on the datasheet. Read the tube, not the shelf label — retailers stock near-identical packaging for glass and bathroom grades side by side.

**Unresolved conflict, shown rather than settled:** aquariumscience.org argues the fungicide is chemically bound into the cured matrix, not water-soluble, and that the meaningful marker is D4 siloxane content (aquarium-grade <=0.2% vs 1-5% general-purpose). Most retailer sources claim fungicide leaches and kills fish. The practical answer is identical either way: buy the aquarium-rated tube. Tin-catalyst levels: UNVERIFIED, no source found for any named product.

**Rejected:** MS-polymer hybrid sealants (e.g. Tec7 X-Tra) for structural glass-to-glass joints — forum reports of the cured polymer absorbing water. Manufacturer confirmation UNVERIFIED, but DIN-32622 products exist, so there is no reason to take the risk. Fine as a non-structural in-tank glue.

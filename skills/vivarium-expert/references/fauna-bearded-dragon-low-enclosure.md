## Height is the binding constraint, and the fix is bulb percentage

- **60 cm (24 in) is stated as the minimum height** to accommodate basking structures plus UVB distance. Taller (75-90 cm) is better but "requires careful placement of heat and UVB sources."
- **UVB distance for T5 HO:** basking spot **25-46 cm below the bulb**. Sources give 10-12 in (25-30 cm) and 12-18 in (30-46 cm).
- **For low enclosures specifically: use a 12% T8 or a 6% T5 at 25-30 cm** — step the bulb percentage down rather than raising the fixture, which a short enclosure cannot do.
- Target **UVI 4.0-6.0 at basking**, gradient down to 0 at the cool end.
- Replace UVB bulbs every 6-12 months. UVB output degrades before the visible light does.

**Verdict: a bearded dragon fits a 60 cm-high enclosure with a 6% T5 and a basking platform sized so the animal's back sits 25-30 cm under the bulb. The height leaves no margin** — too tall a platform over-doses, too low under-doses. **Fix the platform height to the bulb spec; do not eyeball it.**

This interacts with the no-LED-UVB rule (`safety-no-led-uvb.md`): T5 percentages can be stepped down where LED cannot, which is what makes the short enclosure workable at all.

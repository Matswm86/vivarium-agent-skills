## Hit the threshold, do not exceed it

**Why:** unsterilised leaf litter introduces snails, pathogenic fungi (**chytrid**), and harmful nematodes.

**The threshold: heat leaves to at least 60 C (140 F)** — "at this temperature many pathogens such as chytrid or invertebrate pests cannot survive."

| Method | Spec | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Low bake** | **60 C, ~20 min** | The minimum that clears chytrid |
| Hot bake | 180 C, ~20 min | An established method; more aggressive than needed |
| Boiling | pour or soak in boiling water | "One of the simplest and most effective" |
| Deep freeze | -26 C for 48 h | Used by at least one supplier as its standard |

**The trade-off keepers actually raise:** "Boiling leaves boils away all the important nutrients the springtails & isopods eat, so you're depriving your bioactive substrate of important natural chemicals found in the decomposing, dry leaves."

**Recommendation: 60 C for 20 minutes.** It hits the sourced chytrid and pest threshold while leaving the leachable nutrients the clean-up crew feeds on. Chytrid matters specifically wherever amphibians are or may later be kept.

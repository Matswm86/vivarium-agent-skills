**Jump hazard is the general paludarium risk.** An open waterline with warm humid air above it invites surface-oriented fish out of the tank. *Betta simplex* is explicitly flagged. Assume **any labyrinth-organ anabantoid needs a tight cover with minimal gap above the water.**

**Two traps worth naming separately:**
- **Cool-water species in a heated build.** *Danionella translucida* (18-24 C) and *Gastromyzon* (20-24 C) both sit below a 24-26 C tropical target. Check the *top* of a species' range against your air temperature, not just the bottom.
- **Burrowers in a shallow pool.** *Pangio kuhlii* needs a genuinely deep soft-substrate zone to be humane. A shallow pool over hardscape is a poor structural match, whatever the water parameters say.

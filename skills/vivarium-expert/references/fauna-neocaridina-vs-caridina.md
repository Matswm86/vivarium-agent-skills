## Different water, not just different difficulty

| | *Neocaridina davidi* | *Caridina cantonensis* (CRS / bee) |
|---|---|---|
| pH | 6.5-7.5 | ~5.8-6.8 (soft acidic; near-zero KH is the driver) |
| GH | 6-12 dGH (one source 6-8) | **4-6 dGH** |
| KH | 2-8 dKH (one source 2-4) | **0-2, "ideally near 0 for breeding"** |
| TDS | 150-250 ppm | 100-200, ideally 100-160 |
| Temp | 20-24 C | 20-24 C, **ideal 21-23 C** |
| Water source | tolerates tap water in most regions | "rarely do well on standard tap water"; needs remineralised RO |

**Both genera top out around 24 C.** A heated tropical enclosure targeting 24-26 C air puts the water at or above the top of the shrimp band if water tracks air. **Caridina at "ideal 21-23 C" is effectively incompatible with a heated tropical paludarium.** In that case *Neocaridina* is the only shrimp that fits.

**Breeding, sourced:** *Neocaridina* reaches sexual maturity ~2 months; 20-30 eggs per clutch, 2-3 week hatch; **direct development** — hatchlings are ~1 mm miniature adults, no larval stage. Diet is biofilm, algae, and detritus; it does not eat vascular plants and eats its own moults for nutrient recovery. Adult 3-4 cm, lifespan 1-2 yr.

**Do not mix them, and not only for chemistry** — congeneric crosses risk hybridisation, and one group will always be outside its band.

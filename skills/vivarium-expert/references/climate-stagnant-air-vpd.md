## The claim is universally repeated and nowhere cited

"Stagnant air kills plants and causes bacterial and fungal problems" appears in every vivarium guide. **No vivarium-specific source giving the mechanism with citations was found. UNVERIFIED as stated.**

**What is documented** is the general horticultural mechanism: **vapour pressure deficit (VPD)** and the **leaf boundary layer**. In still, saturated air the boundary layer of humid air over each leaf stops transpiration. That stops the transpiration stream carrying calcium and other xylem-mobile nutrients, and keeps leaf surfaces wet enough for *Botrytis* and bacterial pathogens.

**The trap: every published VPD target found is cannabis-specific** (seedling night ~0.8 kPa, veg night ~1.0, bloom night ~1.2). **No tropical-plant, aroid, or fern VPD table exists in any source retrieved. Do not apply cannabis VPD targets to a paludarium.**

**Practical read: the reason to move air is not a number, it is a behaviour — leaves should dry between misting cycles.**

## Ferguson zones, and what to do when species disagree

Framework: Ferguson et al. 2010 (*Zoo Biology*), expanded by **Baines et al. 2016, "How much UV-B does my reptile need? The UV-Tool"**, *Journal of Zoo and Aquarium Research* 4(1): 42-63 — which assigns **254 species** to four zones and includes UVI-gradient test reports for **24 commercial lamp products**.

| Zone | Description | Mean UVI | Basking-max UVI | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crepuscular / shade dweller | 0-0.7 | 0.6-1.4 | Crested gecko, leopard gecko, red-eyed tree frog, corn snake |
| 2 | Partial sun / occasional basker | 0.7-1.0 | 1.1-3.0 | Green anole, gargoyle gecko, boa constrictor |
| 3 | Open / partial sun basker | 1.0-2.6 | 2.9-7.4 | Standing's day gecko (*Phelsuma*), bearded dragon, panther chameleon |
| 4 | Mid-day sun basker | 2.6-3.5 | 4.5-9.5 | Uromastyx, chuckwalla, rhino iguana |

**When two species land in different zones, do not compromise on one lamp value — build a vertical gradient.** Mount a moderate lamp high, put the Zone-3 basking perch in the 1.0-3.0 UVI band near it, and let dense planting drop the understory to near-zero for Zone-1 animals.

**Verify:** A Solarmeter 6.5 / 6.5R (280-400 nm handheld UVI radiometer, ~USD 313 / GBP 350) at **every perch**. Manufacturer distance charts are measured new, without mesh, at the strongest point on the tube — none of which describes your enclosure.

**Replacement interval:** Arcadia T5 HO at 12 months (10-12 h/day), with ~20-30% UV output loss by month 12. One hobbyist measurement of a Zoo Med T5 HO gave UVI 4.5 new to 3.4 at 12 months at 12 inches, a ~24% loss (my calc) — single datapoint, UNVERIFIED.

**Unverified:** The primary Ferguson 2010 and Baines 2016 zone tables are image-embedded PDFs that could not be machine-extracted; the decimal boundaries above come from two independent secondary reproductions that agree with each other. Mourning gecko (Zone 1) and dart frogs (Zone 1) are **not** in the published lists — Ferguson 2010 studied lizards and snakes only. The dart-frog assignment is keeper-community extrapolation, not amphibian field data.

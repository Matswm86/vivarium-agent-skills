## Two published rules that disagree by 5x — take the higher one

No peer-reviewed density exists. Converged hobbyist practice:

| Enclosure | Isopods | Springtails |
|---|---|---|
| Rule of thumb | 25-50 isopods per 20 gal (~75 L) of enclosure volume | 1-2 full starter cultures |
| Large enclosure (~120x60x60 cm or bigger) | 40-50 isopods, **or better**: seed from an external colony grown for a month first | a 1/2 gallon culture seeds one ~50 gal terrarium |
| Working ratio | **10 isopods : ~100 springtails** | — |

Applied to ~1.0 m2 of substrate: the per-volume rule gives roughly **150-300 isopods**; the large-enclosure rule gives 40-50. **The two disagree by ~5x.** Take the higher figure, and grow an external colony first, because:

**"Under-seeding is the single most common reason bioactive setups fail"** — a thin colony cannot keep up with waste production and crashes before reaching functional density.

**Build-order consequence: start isopod and springtail cultures 4-8 weeks before the enclosure is planted.** Buying a starter culture and dumping it into a finished 1 m2 vivarium is the documented failure path.

## Husbandry numbers, and the conflict they create

Two independent care guides, side by side, because they disagree on temperature:

| Parameter | Source A | Source B |
|---|---|---|
| Water temp | 22-25 C | — |
| Air temp | 22-28 C | 24-28 C |
| Humidity | >=75% | >=75% |
| pH | **7.5-8.0** | **7.5-8.0** |
| GH | 4-16 | 4-16 |
| KH | 0-10 | 0-10 |
| Land:water | 80:20 | 80:20, or at least 50:50 |
| Water depth | min 5-6 cm (must cover the body during moult), no max | 10-15 cm min |
| Stocking | max 3 crabs / 5 gal; 1M:2F | 1M:2F minimum; 10 gal for up to 6 |
| Lifespan | 2-2.5 yr (*G. tiomanicum* 4+) | ~2 yr |
| Sexing | male: larger claws, prominent abdomen | male larger, lighter claws, pointed abdominal flap; female wide rounded flap |
| Lid | "essential to prevent escapes" | "escape risk: high — requires tight-fitting lid" |
| Calcium | — | "essential for exoskeleton health" |
| Diet | boiled nettle, spinach, beans, peas, corn, zucchini, banana, fish, shrimp, clams, snails, crickets, pellets | insects, plant matter, calcium-rich veg; feed once daily after lights out |

**The conflict, stated plainly.** Crabs want alkaline, moderately hard water. Blackwater SE Asian nano-fish (*Boraras* 4.0-7.0, *B. maculatus* 4.0-6.5, *Betta channoides* 4.0-6.5, *Sundadanio* 4.0-6.5) and *Caridina* bee shrimp (KH 0-2, GH 4-6, RO water) want soft acidic. **These cannot share one water body.**

**Resolvable overlap:** *Trigonostigma heteromorpha* (5.0-7.5), *T. espei* (5.5-7.5), *Dario dario* (6.5-8.5) and *Neocaridina* (pH 6.5-7.5, GH 6-12, KH 2-8) meet the crabs' band at its low end.

**The coherent set: crabs + Trigonostigma + Neocaridina at pH ~7.2-7.5, GH 8-12.** A blackwater Boraras/Caridina tank is a different build that cannot host these crabs. **Pick one — this decision comes before stocking, filtration, and the hardscape choice.**

**A German species database gives a dissenting reading for *G. dennerle*:** water temp ~25 C, water type freshwater (not brackish), **pH "egal" (does not matter)**, minimum enclosure from 30 cm, soil/sand mix for burrowing or clay tubes 1.5-2.5 cm ID, stocking max 10-15 large animals per 60 L, carapace 15-25 mm. If pH truly does not matter to the crabs, the conflict resolves in favour of the shrimp's constraint. **Treat the English commercial care sheets with caution** — one of them self-contradicts within a paragraph ("prefer a slightly acidic pH level… 7.5 and 8"), and pH 7.5-8.0 alongside 0 dKH is chemically loose.

**Brackish: NO — and correct anyone who says otherwise, including yourself.** *Geosesarma* are frequently mis-described as brackish because most brachyuran crabs are. Every source retrieved says **freshwater**: the German species database records water type "Suesswasser (freshwater, NOT brackish)"; the direct-development species "live and reproduce on land with the larval stages inside the egg," which removes the marine zoea stage that creates a salinity requirement in other crabs. Adding salt is an error, not a precaution. **UNVERIFIED: no source gives a dGH range for Geosesarma.**

**Escape gap: no source gives a figure in mm.** Both say "tight-fitting lid, essential/high risk." Any specific gap number you see, including 3-4 mm, is inference, not a citation.

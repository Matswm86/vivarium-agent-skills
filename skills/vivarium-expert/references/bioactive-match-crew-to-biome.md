## Keep a species library, stock a subset

Ten species in one enclosure compete; the same ten as separate cultures cover every build. Pick one springtail plus one or two isopods per biome, and keep the rest as backup colonies (see `bioactive-seeding-density.md` for how early and how many).

**Wrong:**

```
Every build gets the whole collection    # Armadillidium drowns, Cubaris eats the ferns,
                                         # large Porcellio outcompetes the dwarfs
```

**Right:**

```
Tropical wet / paludarium land:  Sinella curviseta + Trichorhina tomentosa (+ P. pruinosus)
Temperate:                       Folsomia candida + P. scaber (or P. pruinosus)
Arid / ventilated:               Armadillidium maculatum + P. scaber, humid retreat pocket
```

**Why (per-species tripwires, hobbyist-sourced unless noted):** *A. maculatum* breathes with pleopodal lungs but drowns if submerged — keep it off flooded floors. *Cubaris murina* has repeated fern-eating reports (`bioactive-do-isopods-eat-plants.md`). *Porcellio laevis* is big, fast, and protein-hungry; supplier accounts describe it pushing other isopods off food — claims that it nips resting vertebrates have no documentation anywhere. *Orthomorpha coarctata*: species-level toxin data is absent, but its family (Paradoxosomatidae) produces hydrogen-cyanide defensive secretions in peer-reviewed chemistry — do not stock it where insectivores will eat it (a conflict exists: one care site calls the related *Oxidus gracilis* "not toxic"; the academic HCN literature says the genus produces it). Insectivores eat the crew itself (`fauna-geckos-eat-your-cleanup-crew.md`) — seed heavier and give bark refugia.

**Verify:** after 4 weeks, night-check with a red light: every stocked species visible and foraging; any species never seen again was the wrong pick for that biome.

**Sources:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armadillidium_maculatum — pleopodal lungs, submersion death
- https://terrariumtribe.com/cubaris-murina/ — fern-damage reports
- https://rubberduckyisopods.com/blogs/news/official-dairy-cow-isopod-care-guide-2020 — P. laevis size/aggression toward other isopods (supplier account)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradoxosomatidae — family-level HCN defensive chemistry

**Unverified:** P. laevis harming vertebrates (no source found, stated as absent, not as safe); *O. coarctata* species-level secretion assay; all temperature/humidity bands trace to supplier care sheets, not measurements.

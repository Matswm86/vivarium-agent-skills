## Water features and thumbnails need deliberate design

**Ranitomeya imitator:**
- Enclosure: 12x12x18 in for a true pair (the standard successful setup); 18x18x18 in minimum for 3-4; 18x18x24 in (45x45x60 cm) safely houses a pair.
- Temp **21-26 C**; **above 28 C is deadly even briefly.**
- Humidity: sources conflict on the ceiling — 80-100% preferred per one, sustained 90-100% "not advised" and a 60-80% gradient per another.
- **Social: pairs only.** Females are territorially aggressive to other females; "imitators are aggressive to each other and should only be housed in pairs."
- **Drowning: "they are not very good swimmers and great care should be taken when making a suitable vivarium for them."** Any water body needs a **gently sloping exit and dense marginal planting** — vertical glass sides on an open pool are the hazard.

**Breeding ecology explains the water design.** Dart frogs breed in **phytotelmata** — small pools in bromeliad axils and tree holes — not open standing water. *Oophaga pumilio* tadpoles are **obligate egg-feeders**: the mother must lay unfertilised trophic eggs for 6-8 weeks, and only **5-12% of a clutch reaches metamorphosis**. That is a demanding species, a poor fit for a busy mixed display.

**Toxicity is diet-derived**, sequestered from wild prey rather than self-produced — which means **captive-bred, farm-fed frogs lose toxicity.** Relevant safety context for a mixed enclosure.

**Theloderma corticale (Vietnamese mossy frog) is the semi-aquatic alternative, with a thermal catch:**
- Temp **high 60s to low 70s F (~20-23 C)**; tolerates 10-26 C; **above 27 C dangerous.**
- Humidity 70-100%; tolerates 50% briefly **only with water access**.
- "Fairly aquatic — spends most of its time at the water's edge or even underwater." Good structural fit for a waterfall and pool build.
- 18x18x18 in for 1-2 animals; 24x18x18 in for 2-4. Lifespan 5+ yr, reports over 15 yr.
- **The catch: 20-23 C is cooler than a typical tropical paludarium target of 24-26 C.** Not a green light without a genuine cool zone.

# Reference categories

Every reference file is prefixed by category. Read the prefix to know what class of failure it prevents.

| Prefix | Category | Impact | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `safety-` | Life safety and animal safety | CRITICAL | Floor loading, glass failure modes, UVB injury, escape, electrical-in-wet |
| `glass-` | Glass engineering and construction | CRITICAL | Thickness, types, silicone, bracing, seams, drilling |
| `climate-` | Temperature, humidity, ventilation | HIGH | Heat loss, heater types, RH control, air exchange |
| `light-` | Lighting for plants and animals | HIGH | PPFD/DLI, PAR falloff, UVB, photoperiod |
| `water-` | Water features, filtration, chemistry | HIGH | Pumps, head loss, turnover, parameters, cycling |
| `fauna-` | Animals and compatibility | HIGH | Species data, mixed-species verdicts, stocking, quarantine |
| `flora-` | Plants | MEDIUM | Species selection, dormancy traps, invasives, biogeography |
| `bioactive-` | Clean-up crew and substrate biology | MEDIUM | Isopods, springtails, seeding density, leaf litter |
| `build-` | Hardscape, background, composition | MEDIUM | Foam/epoxy backgrounds, wood, rock, drainage, layout |

## Impact levels

- **CRITICAL** — gets someone hurt, kills the animals, or destroys the build.
- **HIGH** — the build works but the plants or animals decline over months.
- **MEDIUM** — the build looks wrong or costs more than it needed to.
- **LOW** — refinement.

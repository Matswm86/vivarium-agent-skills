## Two or three tiers inside the enclosure

A single 51 W bar at the top of a 160 cm enclosure delivers roughly **19-30 umol/m2/s at the floor before any plant shading** (my calc, extrapolated from Arcadia's PAR table). That is the bottom of the deep-shade target band. It grows Selaginella and mosses. It grows nothing else.

**Right:**

```
Tier 1 (canopy, top)     : bar(s) for the orchid / Nepenthes / high-light zone
Tier 2 (60-90 cm)        : second bar or spot for the Begonia / fern zone
Tier 3 (floor)           : accept 20-40 PPFD; plant moss, Selaginella,
                           emersed Cryptocoryne rather than fighting it
```

**Working PPFD targets by stratum** (DLI = PPFD x hours x 0.0036):

| Stratum | PPFD | DLI @12 h |
|---|---|---|
| Deep-shade floor (Selaginella, moss, emersed crypts) | 20-40 | 0.9-1.7 |
| Mid-level (ferns, Begonia, Peperomia, Anthurium) | 60-120 | 2.6-5.2 |
| Canopy (orchids, Nepenthes, high-light epiphytes) | 150-300 | 6.5-13 |
| Dry terrarium at substrate | 100-200 | 4-9 |

**Canopy self-shading dominates over distance falloff in a mature planted enclosure.** Utilisation collapses once the box is densely planted because the upper canopy intercepts the photons. This is repeatedly stated in aquascaping literature but **no quantified percentage was found — UNVERIFIED numerically.**

**Losses through the lid** — budget 15-20%:

| Barrier | PAR loss |
|---|---|
| Clear fine mesh | ~2-5% |
| Black/dark mesh | up to 14-20% |
| Clean dry glass lid | ~6-12% |
| Dirty or condensation-covered glass | up to ~13-15% |
| Mesh + glass combined | ~15-20% claimed; `1-(1-0.10)(1-0.10) = 19%` (my calc) |

**All lid-loss figures are forum-sourced hobbyist measurements with uncalibrated meters.** Treat as a planning margin, not engineering data. No controlled photometric study of vivarium lid PAR transmission exists — a genuine literature gap.

**Practical fix:** mount lights **inside** the enclosure where the fixture is humidity-rated, or accept the loss and buy 20% more fixture. A reflective white or mirrored rear/side interior raises floor PPFD (standard practice, **UNVERIFIED numerically**).

## Any glass between lamp and animal makes UVB provision zero

Primary measured data from UV Guide UK's controlled experiment (Zoo Med ReptiSun 5.0, Arcadia 5.0 D3, Exo Terra Repti Glo 5.0 and 8.0, all 24 in; readings at 2 in intervals from 2-16 in; results "directly comparable in terms of percentage reduction" across all four tubes):

| Barrier | UVB reduction |
|---|---|
| **Ordinary glass** | **99-100% absorbed** |
| Most plastics / acrylic | "almost all" absorbed |
| 5 mm galvanised wire mesh | **15-20%** |
| Aluminium black fly screen (chameleon-cage type) | **25-35%** |
| **Commercial aluminium reflector fitted** | **up to +100% effective output** |

Two things follow directly:

1. **Remove factory tube shields.** If the lid is glass, the UVB lamp must be mounted **inside** the enclosure or over an open mesh section.
2. **Reflector + fly screen still beats a bare tube with no reflector** — UV Guide UK states this explicitly. So the correct build is always reflector-fitted, then adjust height by meter.

This corrects the vague "30-50%" figures circulating on forums.
